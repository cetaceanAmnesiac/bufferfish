# COMPANTHROFRAC Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-11-04

COMPANTHROFRAC ("we", "our", "the extension") is committed to your privacy (and its creator's). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Chrome Web Store extension.

In this policy, **the ANTHROFRACTAL website** means pages whose URLs match `*://anthrofractal.com/*` or `*://*.anthrofractal.com/*`, covering both HTTP and HTTPS protocols on the root domain and all subdomains.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

COMPANTHROFRAC's sole purpose is to enhance your reading experience of the webcomic [ANTHROFRACTAL](https://anthrofractal.com/). When you visit pages on the ANTHROFRACTAL website, the extension reads:

- The current page URL, to recognize where you are in the comic or website structure.
- Panel image metadata such as title, alt-text, and filename, to collect & present this information in the side panel.
- The Document Object Model (DOM), to read and dynamically manipulate the page layout based on your interface preferences.

The extension processes this information locally to provide its core features. It does not transmit the information it handles to COMPANTHROFRAC's developer or any third party. This information is not used for advertising or analytics.

## 2. Site-Specific Boundaries

COMPANTHROFRAC's site-specific scripts & features operate **exclusively** on the ANTHROFRACTAL website. It can determine whether the active tab is on the ANTHROFRACTAL website or not, but it cannot read the URLs or page content of other sites.

## 3. Local Storage and Data Retention

COMPANTHROFRAC automatically saves your reading progress and user preferences locally on your device using the browser extension's local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). This data may include:

- Panel indices & image identifiers
- Panel title and alt-text, where available
- Which panels have been visited
- Whether a panel's choices have been revealed
- Your custom layout, full-screen settings, etc.

This data is retained until you delete it. You can remove locally stored extension data by uninstalling COMPANTHROFRAC or clearing its stored data in your browser's extension settings.

## 4. Chrome Web Store Policy Compliance

COMPANTHROFRAC's use of information received from the ANTHROFRACTAL website & browser extension APIs adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its explicit Limited Use requirements.
